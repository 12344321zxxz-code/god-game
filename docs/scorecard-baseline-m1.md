## What the M1 baseline says

M1 (snapshot tectonics with template relief) produces no impossible planets, but it shows where M2 has to improve:

- **Ocean floor is too old and too deep.** Every planet's ocean peak sits near −6.1 km (Earth −4.5 km) and median crust age is ~110 Myr (Earth ~60). M1 estimates age from distance to a ridge with a hard 200 Myr cap; M2's spreading and subduction should give a younger, more realistic sea floor.
- **Oldest crust is exactly 200 Myr everywhere** — the cap, not physics.
- **One oversized plate** appears on some 6-plate worlds (up to 63% of the surface) from the random flood fill; force-driven plates in M2 should break these up.
- Shelves (~50 km vs Earth ~80), coastline roughness (D ≈ 1.2) and mountain-belt widths are already in plausible ranges.
